About the show

Two brothers who can’t stop talking about it

The Art of Everything is a podcast hosted by brothers Corbin and Carter Rupe.

We started in 2022 as Art of Artists, a show about what inspires famous musicians, where they came from and how they made it. In 2026 we came back as The Art of Everything. Music is still at the heart of it, and now we follow our curiosity wherever it goes.
